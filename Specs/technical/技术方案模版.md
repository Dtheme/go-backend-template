## 交付状态

> 唯一的机器可读状态，`make check` 中的 `spec-check` 核对：`stage: delivered` 要求 `user_acceptance: confirmed` 且 `review` 为 `pass` 或 `not_required`。用户确认方案后改 `implementing`，进入 Step 6 改 `verifying`，用户在 6.2 明确确认后改 `user_acceptance: confirmed`，Step 7 回写完成后改 `delivered`。

```yaml
stage: planning              # planning | implementing | verifying | delivered
user_acceptance: pending     # pending | confirmed
review: not_required         # not_required | pending | changes_required | pass
```
